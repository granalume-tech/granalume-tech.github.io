granalume privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

The short version

granalume draws your wallpapers on your own computer. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your images never leave your device.

What stays on your computer

  • The wallpapers you create, your gallery, favourites, history and settings are saved only on your computer.
  • If you use the optional iPhone feature, granalume saves a copy of your wallpaper in your own iCloud Drive folder on your computer. Syncing that folder is done by Apple's iCloud, under Apple's privacy policy.

Your purchase

Bought in the Microsoft Store: Windows keeps your Store license, and granalume asks Windows whether this copy was bought. granalume itself sends nothing. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to Store purchases: privacy.microsoft.com.

Bought on Gumroad: when you enter your license key, granalume sends the key, together with the product identifier, to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid. This happens once per computer; after that, granalume never connects again to check your license. Gumroad's privacy policy applies to that purchase: gumroad.com/privacy.

No other information about you or your computer is sent.

Children

granalume does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
